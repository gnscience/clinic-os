What GNScience does

Find. Prevent. Recover. Learn.

A focused set of capabilities across the revenue lifecycle, with AI doing more of the repetitive work and people keeping control over material decisions.

01 · FIND

Locate revenue leakage

Analyze claims, payments, authorizations, coding, and operational signals to pinpoint where earned revenue is being lost.

02 · PREVENT

Catch issues upstream

Flag claim-readiness, authorization, and coding risks earlier, before they turn into denials or manual rework.

03 · RECOVER

Prioritize corrective work

Surface recoverable claims, assemble the evidence, and focus staff attention on the work most likely to pay off.

Improve continuously

Every outcome feeds back into the system, so recommendations get sharper and prevention gets stronger over time.

The platform

An intelligent layer across the systems you already use.

GNScience brings together a healthcare knowledge layer, an operational intelligence layer, and AI workflow agents. Your EHR, practice-management system, clearinghouse, EVV, and payer connections all stay in place.

  • Healthcare knowledge layer. Payer requirements, specialty rules, coding relationships, and evidence paths.
  • Operational intelligence layer. Authorization, visit, documentation, coding, claim, and payment state.
  • AI workflow agents. Analyze, recommend, prepare work, and automate approved actions.

Design Partner Program

See what it would have caught, on your own claims

Our design-partner review costs nothing and changes nothing in your systems. You get a clear picture of where your revenue went.

  1. Business associate agreement

    We sign a BAA before we touch any patient data, and we ask only for what the review needs.

  2. Share a claims and payments extract

    We look back across 18 to 24 months of your claims and payments history.

  3. Get your denial review

    We show which denials our checks would have stopped, what each one cost, and the evidence behind each finding.

  4. Decide what comes next

    Keep the findings either way. If it is worth pursuing, we build the rollout with you.

What design partners get

  • A free historical denial and recovery review
  • Early access to GNScience, shaped around your workflows
  • A direct line to the founders and engineering team
  • Preferred terms when you move to production

What we ask

  • A data extract under a signed BAA
  • A few working sessions with your billing or operations lead
  • Honest feedback on what helps and what does not

John Lee is Co-Founder and CEO of GNScience, where he leads company strategy, commercialization, partnerships, operations, and go-to-market execution. He brings more than 20 years of leadership experience spanning enterprise technology, cloud computing, SaaS, startups, customer solutions, and large-scale program execution.
