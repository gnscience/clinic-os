What it is

Three pillars over one OS

Clinic OS is not a bolt-on widget. It is the operating layer: ontology-network kernel, operational system of record, and packs that specialize without rewriting the core.

01

Ontology-network kernel

A cited knowledge kernel — terms, rules, and evidence paths that agents and humans can inspect. Recommendations carry citations; nothing is a black box.

02

Operational system of record

The record we own: auth, session, credential, visit, and claim-ready state. EMR and clearinghouse remain peripherals. Clinic OS is the SoR for operations.

03

Specialty + capability packs

ABA, home health, pediatrics — and capability packs for revenue integrity and ops intelligence — layered on the same OS. Sequencing is strategy; the architecture is shared.
